# ArenaSPEX - Official Primary PE Curriculum Matrix (2023)

## Authority and method

Primary authority: Ministry of National Education, *Primary Physical Education and Sport Curriculum*, 2023, attached as `docs/EPS.pdf`. PDF and printed page numbers coincide. Source wording below is a concise transcription; spelling is normalized only where the PDF typography makes extraction noisy. Existing ArenaSPEX files are comparison material, not authority.

Provenance labels: `official_verbatim` means wording transcribed from the source; `official_structured_extraction` means source content regrouped without changing meaning; `reviewed_derived` means a documented pedagogical derivation; `platform_interpretation` means existing ArenaSPEX content not established by this PDF; `unresolved` means the evidence is insufficient or conflicting.

## 1. Global curriculum structure

- The primary stage has three phases: awakening/initial learning (Grades 1–2), deepening basic learning (Grades 3–4), and mastery of basic learning (Grade 5). Source: PDF p.8–9, heading `بنية أطوار وكفاءات مرحلة التعليم الابتدائي`. Provenance: `official_structured_extraction`.
- The curriculum defines exactly three domains: `الوضعيات والتنقلات`, `الحركات القاعدية`, and `الهيكلة والبناء`. Source: PDF p.8 (domain definitions), p.10 (15-cell final-competency matrix), p.11–12 (resource matrix). Provenance: `official_verbatim`.
- Domain 1 concerns sound body postures and locomotion in relation to health, immediate surroundings, and the situation. Domain 2 concerns acquiring and integrating basic motor actions such as walking, running, throwing, and jumping. Domain 3 concerns structuring the practice space, organized movement, durable activity, cooperation, rules, and increasingly collective sport. Source: PDF p.8. Provenance: `official_structured_extraction`.
- Each grade programme presents one overall competency, values/attitudes, transversal competencies, then a table per domain containing final competency, competency components, cognitive resources, transversal resources, and allocated time. Source: PDF p.13–24. Provenance: `official_structured_extraction`.
- The programme gives evaluation guidance: evaluate evolving motor behavior through criteria and indicators, not isolated performance. It provides a blank evaluation grid but no complete grade-by-domain criterion/indicator set. Source: PDF p.27–28, headings `توجيهات تتعلق بالتقويم` and `شبكة تقويم الكفاءات الختامية`. Provenance: `official_structured_extraction`.

## 4. Criteria, indicators, and evaluation

The attached curriculum requires competency evaluation through criteria and indicators and supplies a blank four-row grid (PDF p.27–28). It does **not** enumerate a complete set for each of the 15 cells. Therefore the criterion/indicator strings in `annualPlanReference.ts` are Level-2 transcriptions from another source and cannot be promoted to `official_verbatim` from this PDF. Status: 0 explicit grade-domain sets; one official evaluation framework and one blank model. Criteria/indicators remain Annual Plan/reference/evaluation knowledge and are not Learning Section print content.

## 5. Session semantics and remediation

- The PDF organizes competencies/resources and recommends formative assessment, but does not prescribe ArenaSPEX’s exact Diagnostic/Learning/Integration/Summative session sequence. That sequence is `platform_interpretation` or `reviewed_derived`, not verbatim curriculum content.
- “Integration introduces no new learning” is pedagogically compatible with competency mobilization, but the exact product rule is `platform-reviewed`; the PDF does not state it as a session algorithm.
- Remediation is supported as part of formative regulation and differentiated learning activity (PDF p.25–27, pedagogical/evaluation guidance), but no independent remedial lesson type is prescribed. The current “embedded remediation” rule is `reviewed_derived`, not `official_verbatim`.

## 6. Grade 5 run/jump/throw determination

Grade 5 D1 explicitly includes body posture during running, jumping, and throwing, propulsion sequence across all three, and transition to a jumping or throwing style (PDF p.22–23). Grade 5 D2 separately includes technical dynamics/phases of sprinting, jumping, throwing, and gymnastics (PDF p.23–24). Thus the same motor families legitimately occur in two domains with different contexts. The D1 ObjectiveConcept “الانتقال بين الجري والوثب والرمي” is `DERIVED_NOT_VERBATIM` but source-supported in D1; it is not automatically wrong-domain.

## 7. Source-backed progression matrices

### Domain 1 - postures and locomotion

G1 natural postures and immediate surroundings → G2 timely adjustment of posture/movement → G3 composing graded running/throwing operations to the situation → G4 connected individual/group running with body and pace control → G5 adapting linked posture and locomotion across individual and collective sport. Sources: PDF p.13, 15, 17, 19–20, 22–23.

### Domain 2 - basic movements

G1 integrated basic actions and transitions → G2 simple natural actions in varied postures → G3 paced running and throwing under safety rules → G4 coordinated jumping, throwing, and gymnastics in available space → G5 technically sound running, jumping, throwing, and gymnastics. Sources: PDF p.14, 16, 18, 20, 23–24.

### Domain 3 - structuring and construction

G1 use of practice space and formations → G2 selecting space/method to use and preserve tools → G3 organizing interventions through landmarks, target throwing, and rules → G4 relating movement/jump/throw to safe available space → G5 team games, basic tactics, techniques, and rules. Sources: PDF p.14, 16, 18, 21, 24.
